The problem

The information isn’t.

Most trade firms are not short of jobs. They are short of follow-up. Work arrives from everywhere. Then the details live in six places and one person’s memory.

That is why quotes go out late, enquiries go quiet, and the owner is still at the kitchen table at half nine.
